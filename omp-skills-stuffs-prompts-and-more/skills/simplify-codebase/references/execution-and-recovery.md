# Execute and recover a simplification

Use after a scoped change is authorized and the investigation supports the proposed cut. Preserve the actual candidate/base and unrelated user work. If called by a factory task, keep its frozen module/interface and machine result contract.

## Complete the cut

Remove the retired responsibility through the relevant declarations, fields/schemas, dispatch, state/lifetimes, selectors, configuration, imports, generated artifacts, dependency declarations, tests, and current documentation. Do not delete a retained consumer's contract to make a search clean. Regenerate artifacts only through their established owner/tooling. Preserve compatibility and historical inputs still required for recovery.

Separate cuts when they can be independently understood and verified; keep coupled removals together where splitting would break their shared contract. A cross-branch finding must be checked against its original base and the destination candidate. File overlap alone does not establish semantic conflict, and disjoint files do not establish independence.

## Validate the surviving behavior

Use the original detector or analyzer, residue searches, the smallest decisive behavioral proof, and the affected existing repository gates. Broaden checks only for a changed boundary, failure, or unresolved concern. Compare behavior at the actual public contract; derive expectations independently. Reuse an existing regression test when it proves the required distinction.

Remove redundant or implementation-mirroring tests only when adequate proof remains for retained behavior. Distinguish a test of deleted behavior from a test exposing accidental contract loss. A required failure can mean the cut was wrong: restore the affected work or redesign the cut, preserving unrelated edits. Correct a faulty oracle only with grounded evidence and independent recheck; never relabel a required failure as success.

When a changed gate or validator supplies the verdict, use the [verifier checks in TDD](../../tdd/SKILL.md). This adds no new factory check, test quota, or bypass. For review findings and selective replay, use [execution and review](../../engineering-workflow/references/execution-and-review.md).

## Close the findings

Give each material finding a disposition: implemented, retained, consolidated into a named finding, rejected with evidence, superseded, or unresolved with the decisive missing fact and owner. Explain the obligations actually removed, surviving behavior proved, and material limits. Do not claim full coverage for unread domains or treat no safe cut as failure.

Return a caller-owned substep's scoped artifact and proof to the caller. Independent review, integration, release, and maintained learning remain with the existing engineering owner. Use its authorized delivery route rather than starting a second pipeline.
